## AI-supported educational intervention

The AI-supported educational intervention was delivered through a researcher-configured Custom GPT accessed by students through a web browser within the ChatGPT environment. The Custom GPT was configured using the standard Custom GPT setup workflow. No separate web application, custom user interface, or researcher-developed integration with the OpenAI API was developed or used. The researcher entered the intervention instructions and educational content into the Custom GPT configuration fields through the standard setup interface.

The intervention consisted of six synchronous 90-minute sessions. During the first 60 minutes, students in both groups received the same educational component from the same instructor. During the final 30 minutes of each session, students in the intervention group interacted with the Custom GPT using one of six standardized acute myocardial infarction (AMI) clinical scenarios, with one scenario assigned to each session. The AI-supported activity followed three predefined phases: (1) simulated-patient role-play and history-taking, during which clinical information was progressively disclosed in response to students' questions; (2) clinical reasoning and decision-making, during which students summarized and interpreted clinical findings, identified priorities, and justified their decisions; and (3) structured formative feedback and debriefing using a predefined eight-section framework.

The Custom GPT was configured with instructions designed to maintain consistency with the assigned clinical scenario, prevent the fabrication or alteration of clinical findings, discourage premature diagnostic conclusions, and challenge unsafe or incomplete reasoning through focused prompts. The intervention materials, including the system-level instructions, phase-specific prompt architecture, six standardized AMI scenarios, student inquiry–response matrices, clinical reasoning branches, structured feedback rubric, session schedule, and fidelity procedures, are publicly available in the GitHub repository:

https://github.com/rezawest10/AI-AMI-Clinical-Reasoning-Intervention

AI-generated feedback was used exclusively for formative educational purposes. It was not used to calculate, replace, or supplement any formal study outcome measure. Because the intervention was delivered through a hosted generative AI service, exact wording may vary across sessions and over time. Accordingly, reproducibility was defined at the level of the intervention configuration, scenario content, interaction sequence, session timing, and feedback framework rather than identical token-by-token outputs.
